01 // BIO

Bridging RF Hardware & Wireless Architecture

Hardware systems engineer focused on 5G NR infrastructure, embedded systems, and signal processing. As Co-Founder of Nuke Labs, I lead STEM education and hardware development for drones, IoT, and embedded platforms. My work at the IIST 5G Use Case Lab involved live 5G NR infrastructure including gNB, AMF, SMF, UPF, and MEC. I hold a Qualcomm 5G Certified certification in 5G wireless technologies and serve as Chairperson of the IEEE SPS Student Branch Chapter.

I have designed custom ESP32-based flight controllers, built DIY radio transmitters using ESP-NOW, and developed radar-enabled search-and-rescue rovers. My work spans from sensor integration and PID control loops to MEC-based monitoring pipelines and DSP algorithms. I also founded DeepTech Kerala, a community-driven deep-tech innovation hub.
