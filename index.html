About

I am an Electronics & Communication Engineering graduate with hands-on experience in embedded systems, 5G networks, wireless communications, drone development, and signal processing. I worked with live 5G NR infrastructure at the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST) and hold a Qualcomm certification in 5G wireless technologies.

I am an expert in AI-augmented development, leveraging AI agents (opencode, Antigravity) to extract maximum output with minimal resources across firmware, software, and system design.
